SemanticCut AI: Automated Multilingual Video Production Pipeline

Intelligent End-to-End AI Video Engineering System

A cutting-edge Python & AI automation pipeline that cuts hours of manual video editing into minutes. It ingests raw episode video footage and a summarized text script, automatically producing high-retention multilingual short-form videos (Shorts/Reels) in 4 languages: English, Arabic, Spanish, and Portuguese.

⚠️ The Challenge / Problem

Manually editing and summarizing cartoon episodes demands hours of tedious work: slicing silences, millisecond subtitle alignment, scouring raw footage for the right scene to match each line, and engineering comedic sound effects.

💡 The Solution

This system converts raw episode footage + a summarized text script into an authentic, multi-track Adobe Premiere Pro project (XML) with a single command. It harnesses Google Gemini for conversational context understanding and semantic scene matching (Semantic Video Mapping), generates voiceovers in 4 languages, extracts millisecond-accurate synchronized captions via WhisperX, and overlays comedic sound effects automatically (AI SFX). The result? Viral-ready content with near-zero human intervention.

AI TRANSCRIPTION & MAPPING

STAGE 2 · AI Transcription & Phoneme Alignment

High-precision audio extraction and phoneme-level subtitle alignment powered by WhisperX Large-v3 running locally on an RTX 4070 (float16) to generate millisecond-accurate word boundaries.

WhisperX Large-v3 RTX 4070 float16 raw_transcript.srt Phoneme Forced Alignment
03
CORE ENGINE (auto_maker.py)

STAGE 3 · The Core Automation Engine (auto_maker.py)

The primary orchestration hub executing three key operations simultaneously: subtitle scaling, fast voiceover generation, and intelligent comedic sound effect mapping.

3A · Subtitle Scaling

CapCut-style forced alignment (Stable-Whisper for AR, WhisperX for other languages) + mathematical speed division into millisecond maps.

3B · Fast Voiceover

Human audio or Google Gemini TTS tuned for SpongeBob accent, routed through Fast Pacing Engine (Pydub silence trim + FFmpeg pitch-kept speedup).

3C · AI Comedy SFX

Groq API (Llama 3.3 70B) analyzes script comedy beats, classifying gags and mapping to the cartoon SFX soundbed automatically.

04
THE AI COGNITIVE BRAIN (video_mapper.py)

STAGE 4 · The AI Cognitive Brain: Semantic Video Mapping (video_mapper.py)

The intellectual breakthrough of the pipeline: utilizing Google Gemini with high-temperature shot reasoning to match raw video frames with target voiceover narrative beats.

PREMIERE PRO XML ASSEMBLY

STAGE 5 · Premiere Pro XML Assembly & Multi-Track Output
